YANTA keeps your notes, your calendar and your ideas in one app — so the thing you wrote down sits right next to the day it belongs to.

WORKS WITHOUT INTERNET
Everything runs on your phone. Trains, planes, dead zones: you keep writing. A connection is for syncing, not for working.

ONE APP INSTEAD OF SIX
• Markdown notes with checklists, tables and links between notes
• Calendar with reminders, recurring events and .ics import
• A daily journal: capture a thought in one tap, it lands in today's note
• Drawings and sketches right inside your notes
• Reading list for RSS feeds, podcasts and YouTube channels
• Search across everything
• Home-screen widgets for your calendar and quick capture

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your notes are encrypted on your device before they are synced — we cannot read them
• App lock with password, fingerprint or face; what YANTA stores on the phone is encrypted too
• Private folders with a password of their own
• Guest mode for a computer that is not yours, and remote wipe for a lost device
• Optional two-factor sign-in for your YANTA Cloud account
• Open source (AGPL-3.0): anyone can check these claims

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Use YANTA without any account and keep everything on the phone, sync it through your own Google Drive or a folder, or use YANTA Cloud for zero-setup sync across your devices.

AI ASSISTANT, ON YOUR TERMS
Ask questions about your notes, plan your week or draft a text. You decide what the assistant may see: notes, folders and calendars can be hidden from it, and private folders never reach it. Bring your own API key if you prefer.

YANTA is free to use. No ads, no tracking.
